About Hema Bakery

A family bakery serving Vijayawada since 2013, making celebrations sweeter one cake at a time.

Our promise

Love & care in every creation

At Hema Bakery, we believe a cake is more than dessert — it's part of a memory. From a child's birthday to a family anniversary, we work to make every order feel personal.

Since 2013, our family bakery has focused on thoughtful designs and cakes that bring people together. Share your idea with us and we'll help you plan a cake for your occasion.

  • Family bakery, established in 2013
  • Custom designs for your celebration
  • Personalised cakes and messages
  • Based in Kasthuribhaipet, Vijayawada
