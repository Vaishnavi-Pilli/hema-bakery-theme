HEMA BAKERY WEBSITE
===================

Files:
- index.html       Home page
- about.html       About page
- products.html    Product gallery
- story.html       Our Story page
- order.html       WhatsApp order form
- contact.html     Contact page
- styles.css       Responsive styles
- script.js        WhatsApp form behavior

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open index.html in a browser.

HOW TO UPLOAD TO HOSTING
1. Open your hosting File Manager.
2. Open public_html (or the folder for your domain).
3. Upload all HTML files, styles.css and script.js into the same folder.
4. Visit your domain. index.html should load as the homepage.

IMPORTANT CUSTOMISATIONS
- Phone number is set to 7989839636 in script.js and in the HTML phone links.
- Instagram URL is set to https://www.instagram.com/hema_bakery/
- The location and business details are based on the details supplied in the conversation.
- Product prices are not invented; the website asks customers to request a quote.
- Images are remotely hosted by Unsplash and require internet access. Replace image src URLs with your own bakery photos for the best results.
- WhatsApp order forms open a pre-filled WhatsApp message. They do not store orders in a database or automatically confirm orders.
- Before publishing, verify the phone number, exact address, opening hours, delivery options and Instagram link.
