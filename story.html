Our journey

A family tradition, baked with love

Every cake has a story. We are honoured when you let Hema Bakery be part of yours.

Since 2013

Small celebrations. Big memories.

Hema Bakery began with a simple idea: make the moments that bring families together a little sweeter. Since 2013, our family bakery has been creating cakes for birthdays, anniversaries and meaningful milestones.

We love seeing a customer's idea turn into a cake made especially for them — the colours, the theme, the name on top, and the smile when it's revealed.

Our guiding words remain the same: “From our oven to your table with love & care.”
